Background

I’ve worked across football, squash, basketball, wheelchair basketball, taekwondo, hockey, rugby, cricket, netball, and volleyball, with professional clubs, academies, universities, and national programmes.

Today I work through RedZone Analysis in Bath, providing real-time coding and match analysis for PSA Squash and Bath City FC. I also support UEFA technical observers across the Champions League, and provide analysis for British Wheelchair Basketball and the University of Nottingham.

Past work includes communication analysis for England’s men’s national team with the FA, technical analysis at AFCON and WAFCON, and projects with Liverpool FC U15/U16, Cheltenham Town U18s, Team GB Taekwondo, Hockey Wales, and the RFU’s head contact study.
